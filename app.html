      Redact page

      Drag on the page to cover what must disappear. With the keyboard, focus the page: arrows move the crosshair (Shift for bigger steps), Enter starts a rectangle and Enter again places it; Tab goes through the rectangles, which move with the arrows, resize with Shift and the arrows, and go with Delete. In the downloaded PDF the whole page becomes a picture, so the covered content no longer exists.

      Page numbers and watermark

      In a header or a footer, {page} is replaced by the number of the page and {pages} by the number of pages. The Pages field limits all four to a list such as 2-5, 8.

        Place it on the page

        Hide the blank pages

        Every page has been measured. Move the slider: the pages that would be hidden are dimmed. A page with only faint scan noise counts as blank, a page with a stamp or a signature does not.

          What your files carry

          The file you download is a new document: the properties, embedded files, scripts, bookmarks and layers of your files are not copied into it. The links and comments stay on the pages that are copied, unless you remove them below.
